# Lanterval · key characters (Characters tab of the Codex)
# perso.<id>.<n>: info n about the character, in the order of donnees/personnages.js. One or two sentences, no spoilers.

== perso.kael.1
Apprentice guard of Lanterval, 17, Earth element. Stubborn and protective, he had never left the valley before the lantern began to fade.

== perso.kael.2
He brought the crystal heart back from the depths of the cave, and the lantern shone again.

== perso.kael.3
At the top of the pass, the Order took the amber crystal from him and the Nameless struck him down with a single blow.

== perso.kael.4
His father went off into the mist one night and never came back. Brann gave him his sword, too damaged to fight with.

== perso.kael.5
In Salvent, the lantern shard showed him a vision: Orvan at twenty, beside a woman, before a huge eye carved in stone.

== perso.kael.6
In the crater of the Sleeping Volcano, he broke the Nameless's mask. Underneath, he recognised his father, Aldric, who no longer remembers him.

== perso.kael.7
Hilde reforged his father's sword and engraved a lantern on the guard. Kael carries it so that Aldric might recognise it one day.

== perso.kael.8
He gathered the four pages of his father's notebook. Aldric didn't leave out of cowardice: he was following the Order to keep it away from Lanterval.

== perso.mira.1
Daughter of Hilde the blacksmith, 18, Fire element. She was following the mist alone in the forest when Kael pulled her out of a ring of wolves.

== perso.mira.2
She was the one who worked out that the mist was pouring out of the Crystal Cave.

== perso.mira.3
The forges of Cendre, where the Order took the amber crystal, are in her mother's home town.

== perso.mira.4
In the Red Mines, she learned that her mother forged the Order's first cage, then fled with her when she was a baby.

== perso.mira.5
She helped her mother reforge Aldric's sword, all night long, without a word. Between them, something is starting to mend.

== perso.mira.6
With her mother, she threw the mould of the first cage into the volcano. Together they forged the Forgeheart, and they laughed.

== perso.sol.1
Hermit of the underground lake, 20, Water element. Calm and quiet, he had guarded the lake since the Crystal Guardian woke up.

== perso.sol.2
In Salvent, Maëlle recognised him: he is the child of a shipwreck, the one everyone thought lost at sea.

== perso.sol.3
He was six at the time of the shipwreck, and his little sister two. According to the wreck's logbook, a little girl was taken in at Salvent: she is alive.

== perso.sol.4
He found his sister without telling her anything: she remembers nothing and has just got her adoptive father back. He chose to stay with Kael and Mira.

== perso.sol.5
Their mother's lullaby gave Naïa back the memory of her brother. She gave him the Staff of Tides, fished out of the wreck.

== perso.orvan.1
The Elder of Lanterval, keeper of the lantern. He gave Kael a shard of its crystal.

== perso.orvan.2
He recognised the closed-eye badge: the symbol of the Order of the Eyelid, which vanished fifty years ago.

== perso.orvan.3
He admitted that the crystal heart would only last a season, and sent the team to find the amber crystal at the Stone Pass.

== perso.orvan.4
In a letter, he admitted he founded the Order of the Eyelid with Ysold fifty years ago, before leaving out of fear.

== perso.brann.1
Lanterval's guard and Kael's captain. A former hunter, he knows the forest and its beasts better than anyone.

== perso.brann.2
He brought the team down from the pass on a cart after their defeat against the Nameless.

== perso.brann.3
Kael's father left him his sword the night he went off into the mist: “For Kael, when he's ready.”

== perso.brann.4
His scouts followed the amber crystal to the forges of Cendre, in the east.

== perso.brann.5
Aldric had left him a page of his notebook, to give to Kael the day he was ready. He kept it in a chest for ten years.

== perso.hilde.1
Mira's mother. She runs the Lanterval forge and sells weapons and armour.

== perso.hilde.2
When she heard about the closed-eye badge, she asked Kael not to show it to anyone else.

== perso.hilde.3
She comes from Cendre, the forge town, and fully intends to go there with the team.

== perso.hilde.4
She was born in Forgecendre, where people thought she had been dead for eighteen years. She relit her father's old forge there.

== perso.hilde.5
She forged the Order's very first cage, thinking it would protect the crystals. When she understood, she fled with Mira, who was three months old.

== perso.hilde.6
She knew Aldric, Kael's father. Her cart now takes the team from village to village.

== perso.hilde.7
Before fleeing, she locked the mould of the first cage behind a door the Order never managed to open. She fought alongside the team to take it back.

== perso.silhouette.1
A hooded figure who was prowling the forest when the wolves surrounded Mira.

== perso.silhouette.2
It set an ambush in the cave to take back the crystal heart. On its sleeve, the same closed eye as on the badge.

== perso.varen.1
A young man of the Order of the Eyelid, fast and arrogant. At the top of the pass, he took the amber crystal. To him, forgetting your sorrows in the mist is a cure.

== perso.varen.2
The Herald of Salvent knows him: Varen told him about the team.

== perso.varen.3
He is hunting Nev, who left the Order. He is sure she will come back of her own accord.

== perso.varen.4
He is Nev's older brother. He took her into the Order when she was ten, promising she would never hurt again. Kael and Nev beat him in a duel at the crater's edge.

== perso.sansnom.1
A colossus in a steel mask marked with a closed eye, who obeys Varen. At the pass, he struck the team down with a single blow of his mace.

== perso.sansnom.2
Under the broken mask: Aldric, Kael's father, who left into the mist years ago. The Order took his memory and his name. He carried the ember crystal off to the north.

== perso.sansnom.3
His notebook says it: he was following the Order to protect Lanterval, and he knew he might end up behind a mask. He asked Kael not to let him forget his voice.

== perso.maelle.1
The old innkeeper of Salvent, too old, she says, for the mist to find room to settle. She recognised Sol, the child of the shipwreck.

== perso.maelle.2
She revealed that the little girl from the shipwreck was taken in by Erwan, the lighthouse keeper.

== perso.naia.1
Sol's sister, 16, lost in the shipwreck. Erwan, the lighthouse keeper, took her in and raised her in Salvent. She does not remember her family from before.

== perso.naia.2
She hums a lullaby nobody else knows: the one their mother sang every evening.

== perso.naia.3
Her music box, found in the wreck, is broken. It will take a blacksmith's fingers to fix it.

== perso.naia.4
Hearing the lullaby from the repaired box, she remembered Sol, her older brother.

== perso.erwan.1
The keeper of the Salvent lighthouse, held prisoner by the Order. He took Naïa in and raised her as his daughter.

== perso.nev.1
A novice of the Order, on guard at the foot of the lighthouse. Troubled by what the Order is doing to Salvent, she let the team through without raising the alarm.

== perso.nev.2
She fled the Order and joined the team in the Burnt Fields. 16 years old, Wind element. She never wants to go back.

== perso.nev.3
Varen is her brother. She faced him in a duel at the crater's edge, side by side with Kael.

== perso.nev.4
She was born in Aerie, in the High Winds, where the only crystal the Order hasn't taken yet sleeps: the azure crystal.

== perso.heraut.1
The Order officer who held the Salvent lighthouse. He sincerely believes he is offering peace by wiping away sorrow. Defeated, he fled in a boat.

== perso.ysold.1
She founded the Order of the Eyelid with Orvan fifty years ago. After losing her whole family in the war, she wanted to close the Eye, the source of the world's memory.

# ---------- Side quests of the valley ----------

== perso.roxane.1
A hunter who keeps a logbook of the beasts nobody dares to hunt, one per region. She follows the team from inn to inn.

== perso.roxane.2
She learned to hunt from an old master in the High Winds. She says he never taught her to be afraid, only to be careful.

== perso.roxane.3
Three beasts from her logbook have fallen: the Rock Boar, the Thunder Eel and the Salamander Queen. Only the Winged Roc of the High Winds remains.

== perso.fennel.1
A peddler who never sells anything: he trades. He says every village has what another one wants.

== perso.fennel.2
Fennel knows everyone, from Lanterval to the coast. Nobody knows where he comes from, and his answer changes every time someone asks.

== perso.fennel.3
For Dorn's star ingot, he gave up his roc feather, his finest treasure. He has arranged to meet the team in Aerie.

== perso.dorn.1
A blind old miner from Forgecendre. He can't see any more, but he hears everything: the hammers, the rock, and hearts beating too fast.

== perso.dorn.2
Thanks to the singing stone, he found the mountain's voice again, and taught the team the art of the geomancer.

== perso.tobi.1
A Forgecendre miner's son. His father didn't come back up from the mines with the others.

== perso.tobi.2
His father, Ulric, had fled the mines through an old shaft and was hiding in the Burnt Fields. They are together again.

== perso.ysold.2
The monastery frescoes say it: if the Eye closes completely, no one will ever remember anything again. That is what she wants.

== perso.ysold.3
At the top of the Howling Peak, she tore the azure crystal from Varen and abandoned him. She now holds all four crystals.

== perso.roxane.4

== perso.fennel.4
His finest trade is done: from Lanterval honey all the way to the Cloak of the four winds, woven by Ilse in Aerie.

== perso.tavor.1
The last monk of the Aerie monastery. He agreed to guide the team to the azure crystal, despite the village's distrust of Nev.

== perso.tavor.2
He showed the team the frescoes: the Eye is the memory of the world, and the mist is the Eye closing.

== perso.tavor.3
He saw Ysold and Orvan leave the monastery fifty years ago. He never stopped waiting for someone to come and reopen the Eye.

== perso.pell.1
Postman of the High Winds, he flies from village to village by glider. He brought the team news from Salvent.

== perso.pell.2
The team delivered his last letters to Lanterval, Salvent and Forgecendre. He left them his quill, which knows every road.

== perso.ilse.1
A weaver in Aerie, she weaves the glider sails and cares for the last wyverns of the cliffs.

== perso.ilse.2
From a roc feather, she wove the Cloak of the four winds. Fennel is her oldest customer.
