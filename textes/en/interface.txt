== notes.version
## Version 0.3.0
Act 2, “The town that forgets”, is playable: the Salt Marsh, Salvent, the Lantern Marsh, the Sunken Wreck and the Drowned Lighthouse.
The Salvent Coast has its own map. The Salt Marsh links the valley and the coast, and the map menu also offers to go back to Lanterval.
Three bosses (the Marsh Wyvern, the Abyssal Mother, the Herald of the Eyelid), seven new monsters and two side-quest bosses.
New classes: Monk, Thief and Bard, granted by the sea-foam crystal. Secret class: Corsair.
New techniques: Flurry, Meditation, Steal, Dodge, Song of courage, Lullaby, Boarding strike.
Side quests: “The forgotten ship” and “The lost prices”; “The lullaby” begins (it continues in act 3).
At Gaspard's, prices change on every visit: in red when inflated, in green when reduced, until his ledger is found.
Shops have tabs: Weapons, Armour, Accessories and Consumables (when buying and when selling).
After a defeat, the team wakes up at the inn of the last village visited.
Images: everything new is provisional.
## Version 0.2.2
No more buttons that take the team to the quest automatically: a yellow (main quest) or blue (side quest) “!” marks the Map button, the Cross button and the place on the map.
## Version 0.2.1
In the Codex, each class expands with a tap to show its weapons, stats and techniques.
## Version 0.2.0
Act 1 is complete: the Stone Pass is open, with its treasures, events and two new monsters.
At the top of the pass, a duel against Varen, then an encounter with the Nameless that cannot be won (no gold lost).
Kael wakes up at the inn, Brann gives him his father's sword, and the Salt Marsh appears on the map.
Hilde, Mira's mother, runs the village forge: she sells weapons and armour, and new pieces arrive after the celebration.
Berthe now sells herbs and tonics.
## Version 0.1.2
The Codex has a Classes tab: unlocked classes, their weapons, stats and techniques.
The Ember staff is no longer a Fire weapon: the normal Attack stays Blade, with no colour; only techniques with an element keep their colour.
The Team menu shows HP, PP and XP gauges with numbers.
Tap a stat in the Team menu to see what it does; the Codex help explains them all.
The Journal sorts current and completed quests into folding lists; classes are in the Codex, locked ones shown as ???.
Menu windows keep the same size whatever the tab.
Fix: if the page is closed in the middle of a step, the step resumes where it was (a won fight is no longer replayed).
## Version 0.1.1
Text now appears without moving the screen or the buttons.
Tap anywhere to move on in a dialogue.
Monsters are half the size in combat.
Combat icons take the colour of their element.
## Version 0.1.0
First real game, built from the prototype.
Act 1 plays up to the lantern festival, with the alpha wolf side quest.
Autosave, backup copy and game export in the Options.
New game+: start the story over while keeping classes, levels, gear and codex.
The game works offline and in English.
Images: the village, Kael, the wolf and the festival are final; the rest is temporary.
