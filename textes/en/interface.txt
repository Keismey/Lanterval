== aide.tours
*Turn order*, at the top of the screen: the first one, framed in gold, plays now. Speed decides the order.

== aide.garde
The enemy's *guard*. Each hit on a weakness removes 1 point.

== aide.brise
Guard at 0: the enemy loses its next turn and takes +50 % damage. A charged attack is cancelled.

== aide.elements
*Elements*: Blade, Earth, Fire, Water. Each hero has their own (Kael Earth, Mira Fire, Sol Water); the normal Attack is always Blade.

== aide.inconnue
Weakness not discovered yet. Hit it once and it stays shown, here and in the bestiary.

== aide.resistance
Resistance: damage halved.

== aide.union
*Union*: each weakness you hit fills the gauge. At 3, the whole team strikes every enemy.

== aide.chaine
*Chain*: when two heroes play in a row, each hit does +15 % (up to +45 %).

== aide.auto
*Auto*: automatic normal attacks. Turns off if a hero drops below 30 % HP. Not available against bosses or in quests.

== notes.version
## Version 0.1.0
First real game, built from the prototype.
Act 1 plays up to the lantern festival, with the alpha wolf side quest.
Autosave, backup copy and game export in the Options.
New game+: start the story over while keeping classes, levels, gear and codex.
The game works offline and in English.
Images: the village, Kael, the wolf and the festival are final; the rest is temporary.
