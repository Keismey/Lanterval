== notes.version
## Version 0.6.5
Village: characters in two columns, the main quest on top, “Leave” always at the bottom.
Shops: one line per item with its price and what it gives each hero (+ in green, = in grey, ✕ if they can't use it). Tapping an item opens its sheet: hero-by-hero comparison, “Buy and equip” in one go, or ×1, ×5, ×10 for consumables. Selling is now a tab.
Team: compact header, Equipment, Class and Techniques tabs, and an Optimise button that equips the best from the bag.
Inventory: give an item with a single tap on a hero's face; equipment can be filtered by type and shows who it helps.
## Version 0.6.4
All texts proofread: a few story inconsistencies fixed (Cendre and Forgecendre, Nev's age, the mist for fifty years…) and clearer wording, in French and English.
In combat, a lone boss's name is shown in full.
## Version 0.6.3
Five new tracks: Lanterval in the mist (before the lantern is relit), Salvent, Forgecendre, Aerie, and the journey on the world maps (also in the zones, until each region gets its own music).
## Version 0.6.2
End credits after the story ends, before your game summary.
## Version 0.6.1
Lighter first launch: the game starts after about 4 MB, then downloads the rest (pictures and music) bit by bit while you play, so it works offline afterwards. An update now only downloads what changed.
## Version 0.6.0
Lanterval has sound: the main theme on the title screen, the Lanterval village theme, and the battle music. Other places borrow these tracks until they get their own.
Sound effects for buttons, dialogue, windows, hits, weaknesses, broken guards, each element's magic, healing, gold and discoveries.
Options › Music and sound effects: two settings from 0 (off) to 10. Sound starts on your first tap and stops when you leave the game.
## Version 0.5.5
New title screen: the village at peace for “Tap to start”, then the village in the mist behind the buttons (it stays at peace once you've seen the ending).
Continue shows the current act, or “The End” and your completion (zones explored and side quests). New Game + appears once you've seen the ending.
## Version 0.5.4
Start screen: the Keismey Studio logo, then the game's picture and “Tap to start”.
Phone back button: it closes windows one at a time, steps back in shops, battle menus and the map, then offers the title screen. From the title screen, it offers to go to the hub.
The top bar fits on small phones (play time is still shown on the title screen and in the Journal).
## Version 0.5.3
The finale of Lanterval: the Stair of Mist, the Nameless, then Ysold and the Eyelid of the World at the Heart of the Eye. One epilogue per hero, which changes with the side quests you finished.
Before you climb, the game warns you: once on the Stair, no more map or village until the end. After the credits, you come back to Aerie just before the Stair, with everything you earned, and an “ending seen” star.
New side quest after the end: “The Echoes” (Garrick, in Aerie, once the hunter's logbook is complete). The Depths, which led nowhere, leave the valley map.
