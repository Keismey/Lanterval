== a3.titre
The Forges of Cendre

# ---------- Step 1: Burnt Fields ----------

== a3.e1.titre
Cross the Burnt Fields

== a3.e1.bouton
Take the road to Cendre

== a3.e1.lisiere
## Act 3 · The Forges of Cendre
East of Lanterval, the wheat turns grey. Then black. A smell of smoke hangs over fields that burned long ago.
Mira: “The Cendre Plains. My mother never talks about them. She changes the subject as soon as anyone says the name.”
Sol: “Listen. Someone is running, over there.” [Cross the fields]

== a3.e1.nev
## Burnt Fields
A white-haired girl tumbles down a bank of ash. Behind her come Order soldiers and two Shadows.
Kael: “It's the novice from the lighthouse! The one who let us through.”
Nev: “You again? Good. They want to drag me back, and I'm not going.”
Nev: “My name is Nev. We'll talk later.” [Fight]

== a3.e1.varen
## Burnt Fields
A silver-haired man watches from the top of a charred wall. Varen.
Varen: “You pick such weak friends, Nev. I'll let you play for a while. You'll come back on your own.”
Nev: “Never. Do you hear me, Varen? Never!”
Varen vanishes into the smoke.

== a3.e1.ferme
## The burnt farm
Nev catches her breath against a blackened wall.
Nev: “In Salvent, I saw mothers forget their own children. The Order calls that healing. I call it theft.”
Nev: “They take the crystals to Forgecendre, the mining town. I know the way. I'm coming with you.”
Mira: “Forgecendre… So we're going to see where my mother grew up.”

== a3.e1.message
*Nev* joins the team. *Forgecendre* appears on the map of the Cendre Plains.

# ---------- Step 2: Forgecendre ----------

== a3.e2.titre
Discover Forgecendre

== a3.e2.bouton
Enter Goran's inn

== a3.e2.arrivee
## Forgecendre
Forgecendre smokes through a hundred chimneys. Hammers ring day and night, and banners with a closed eye hang from every door.
Grey-faced miners walk by in silence, escorted by sentinels.
Nev: “The whole town works for the Order. Those who refuse go down to the bottom of the mines and never come back up.”

== a3.e2.hilde
## Forgecendre
A cart creaks over the cobblestones. On the bench, a woman with red braids holds the reins.
Mira: “Mum? What are you doing here?”
Hilde: “I told you I'd come. I'm not letting my daughter walk into this town alone.”
An old miner stops and stares at her. “Hilde the smith? We thought you'd been dead for eighteen years.”

== a3.e2.ville
## Goran's inn
Goran: “Hilde's back… Some people here will be glad. And some will be scared.”
Hilde: “I'm not here for old memories, Goran. I'm here for my children. All four of them.”
Kael: “Nev says the stolen crystals are here. Where does the Order keep them?”
Goran: “At the bottom of the Red Mines. They force our miners to forge cages down there.”

== a3.e2.forge
## The forge
Hilde: “My father's old forge is still standing. I'll light it again.”
Hilde: “Come and see me before you go down the mines. Here, I can make much better weapons than in Lanterval.”
Mira looks at her mother without a word. She has a thousand questions, and none of them come out.

== a3.e2.message
Hilde has lit her forge in *Forgecendre*. The *Red Mines* appear on the map.

# ---------- Step 3: Red Mines, the entrance ----------

== a3.e3.titre
Free the miners at the entrance

== a3.e3.bouton
Go down into the mines

== a3.e3.entree
## Red Mines
The rock is red as embers. Rails run down into the dark, and far below, hammers strike in rhythm.
At the entrance, chained miners push iron carts. Sentinels and an automaton watch over them. [Fight]

== a3.e3.mineurs
## Red Mines
The chains fall. A miner points at the tunnel going deeper.
“Thank you… The others are further down, where they cast the cages. And the thing that forges them never sleeps.”
Nev: “The Cage Forger. I saw it once. It was built never to stop.”

== a3.e3.message
More miners are held further down: cross the *Red Mines* to the *deep gallery*.

# ---------- Step 4: Red Mines, Hilde's confession ----------

== a3.e4.titre
Go down to the deep gallery

== a3.e4.bouton
Follow the rails down

== a3.e4.descente
## Red Mines
Hilde is waiting by the rails, a lamp in her hand.
Hilde: “I know these tunnels better than anyone. I'm coming with you all the way down.”
Mira: “Do you know the cages too, Mum?” Hilde doesn't answer. [Go down]

== a3.e4.atelier
## The workshop
Halfway down, a great hall carved into the rock. In the middle, under a dusty sheet, stands a cage of black iron.
Hilde stops in front of it. Her lamp trembles.

== a3.e4.aveu
## The first cage
Hilde: “I forged that one. The very first. The Order promised me it would protect the crystals.”
Hilde: “When I understood it was meant to steal them, I fled in the night. With you, Mira. You were three months old.”
Mira: “You lied to me my whole life.”
Hilde: “I protected you your whole life. It isn't the same… but I know it looks like it.”

== a3.e4.alerte
## The workshop
A hiss of steam. Rock worms crawl out of the walls, and an automaton starts to move. [Fight]

== a3.e4.galerie
## The deep gallery
At the end of the rails, the tunnel opens onto a huge forge. Dozens of miners beat iron under the whip.
In the middle, an iron figure as tall as three men pours metal into a cage-shaped mould.
Mira walks ahead without a glance at her mother.

== a3.e4.message
The *Cage Forger* is waiting in the *deep gallery*.

# ---------- Step 5: the Cage Forger ----------

== a3.e5.titre
Stop the Cage Forger

== a3.e5.bouton
Face the Cage Forger

== a3.e5.forgeur
## The deep gallery
The Cage Forger turns its anvil head towards you. Its eyes light up like furnaces.
Nev: “It was built to obey. Not to think.”
Hilde: “I know that metal. Water cracks it, and wind keeps it from heating. Go for its guard!”

== a3.e5.conseil
The Cage Forger is weak to *Water* and *Wind*, and resists Fire and Blade. It charges big blows against the whole team and can call automatons. Break its guard to cancel a charged blow.

== a3.e5.libres
## The deep gallery
The Forger collapses in a crash of iron. The forge fire goes out all at once.
The miners look up. Then one throws down his hammer, and all the others follow.
“Free! We're free!” The shouts echo all the way to the surface.

== a3.e5.message
The miners are free. Go back up to *Forgecendre*, to Goran's inn.

# ---------- Step 6: Forgecendre, Mira's anger ----------

== a3.e6.titre
Return to Forgecendre

== a3.e6.bouton
Join the miners' celebration

== a3.e6.fete
## Goran's inn
Tonight, Forgecendre celebrates its miners. The Order's banners burn in a barrel, and Goran pours drinks for free.
Goran: “The sentinels fled towards the volcano. All of them. As if something was calling them.”
Nev: “The ember crystal. It sleeps in the crater of the Sleeping Volcano. The Order wants to take it before you get there.”

== a3.e6.mira
## Outside the inn
Mira sits alone on the steps. Hilde comes closer, then stops three paces away.
Hilde: “I'm not asking for anything. I just wanted you to know I'm here.”
Mira doesn't look up. Kael sits down next to her.
Kael: “Do you want to talk about it?”

== a3.e6.choix
## Outside the inn
Mira: “She ran away to protect me, I know that. But she forged the cage that stole our crystal. What should I do, Kael?”
> maintenant : “Talk to her tonight.” | Mira tries to forgive right away
> plustard : “Take your time.” | Mira will wait until she's ready

== a3.e6.maintenant
## Outside the inn
Mira gets up and joins her mother. They stand face to face for a long time.
Mira: “I'm still angry with you. But I don't want to be anymore. Is that a start?”
Hilde: “It's the best start in the world.”

== a3.e6.plustard
## Outside the inn
Mira: “You're right. I'm not ready. Not yet.”
She watches Hilde from afar, who pretends not to see them. Mira gives a small wave, then goes inside.

== a3.e6.volcan
## The next day
A miner draws a map in the ash with his finger.
“The volcano path starts behind the forges. It climbs to the edge of the crater. Watch out for the lizards, they never sleep.”

== a3.e6.message
The *Sleeping Volcano* appears on the map. The ember crystal sleeps in its crater.

# ---------- Step 7: Sleeping Volcano, the slopes ----------

== a3.e7.titre
Climb the Sleeping Volcano

== a3.e7.bouton
Climb towards the crater

== a3.e7.pentes
## Sleeping Volcano
The slope is black and warm underfoot. Lava flows still smoke between the rocks.
Lava lizards guard the path, along with a sentinel who fell behind. [Fight]

== a3.e7.ordre
## Sleeping Volcano
Further up, boot prints in the ash. A lot of boots.
Nev: “They're already at the top. We have to hurry.” [Fight]

== a3.e7.bord
## The crater's edge
The path comes out at the edge of the crater. Below, in the middle of a lake of cooled lava, a red crystal beats like a heart.
Order soldiers are building a cage around it. They haven't finished yet.
Kael: “We have a chance. Just one.”

== a3.e7.message
The ember crystal is at the bottom of the *crater*. The Order hasn't finished caging it.

# ---------- Step 8: the ember crystal, Varen ----------

== a3.e8.titre
Reach the ember crystal

== a3.e8.bouton
Go down into the crater

== a3.e8.cristal
## The ember crystal
You rush down the slope among the startled soldiers. Kael lays his hand on the crystal just before the cage closes.
The heat runs through you without burning. The lantern shard answers, red and gold.
Mira: “I can feel it… It's teaching us something.”

== a3.e8.classes
## The ember crystal
? New classes: *Berserker* (Fury, Rage), *Alchemist* (Corrosion, Mixture) and *Summoner* (Summon, Offering). Change class in the Team menu.

== a3.e8.varen
## The crater
Slow applause. Varen climbs down the rocks, his curved blade in hand.
Varen: “Nice. But you can touch a crystal without getting to keep it.”
Varen: “Nev, come back with me and I'll forget you ever left. As for you, guard, we have a score to settle.”
Nev: “Kael, we do this together. The rest of you, guard the crystal!”

== a3.e8.conseil
Two-on-one duel: *Kael and Nev* against Varen. Varen is weak to *Earth* and *Blade*, and resists Wind. He is very fast and sometimes strikes twice.

== a3.e8.fuite
## The crater
Varen steps back, a hand on his shoulder. He looks at Nev for a long time.
Varen: “You learned to fight. Not from me.” He whistles, and his soldiers fall back to the top of the crater.
Kael: “He left too quickly. As if he knew something else was coming.”

== a3.e8.repos
## The crater's edge
You take shelter in a cave at the edge of the crater. Sol tends the wounds. Mira sits down next to Nev.
Mira: “Varen talks to you as if he'd always known you.”
Nev: “He's my brother. He took me into the Order when I was ten. He said that there, we'd never hurt again.”
Mira: “Families are complicated. I'm starting to learn that.”
? Rest at the crater's edge: the team has recovered all its HP and PP.

== a3.e8.message
Varen has fallen back. Something is coming towards the *crater*.

# ---------- Step 9: the Nameless ----------

== a3.e9.titre
Defend the ember crystal

== a3.e9.bouton
Go back to the crystal

== a3.e9.sansnom
## The crater
The ground shakes. A huge figure climbs down into the crater, a mace on its shoulder. The Nameless.
Kael grips his father's sword. At the pass, he could do nothing.
Kael: “This time there are four of us. And we're not backing down.”

== a3.e9.conseil
The Nameless is weak to *Fire* and *Wind*, and resists Earth and Water. He charges a terrible blow against the whole team: break his guard to cancel it. This time, he can be defeated.

== a3.e9.masque
## The broken mask
Kael's last blow splits the steel mask. It falls in two pieces into the ash.
Beneath it, a tired face, a grey beard, eyes that no longer know where they are.
Kael: “No… Dad?”
The man looks at him without recognising him. “I have no name. I have no son.”

== a3.e9.fuite
## The crater
While Kael stands frozen, the Nameless smashes the cage with his mace and tears out the ember crystal.
He disappears into the smoke, heading north. Nobody follows him.
Mira: “Kael…” Kael doesn't answer. He picks up the two halves of the mask.

== a3.e9.message
The Order has taken the ember crystal. Go back to *Forgecendre*, to Hilde's forge.

# ---------- Step 10: Forgecendre, Aldric's sword ----------

== a3.e10.titre
Go back to Hilde's forge

== a3.e10.bouton
Show the sword to Hilde

== a3.e10.forge
## The forge
Hilde: “Aldric… I knew your father, Kael. A man who laughed loudly and never gave up.”
Hilde: “If the Order stole his memory, he'll need to recognise something. Give me his sword.”
Hilde stokes the forge. Mira takes the bellows without being asked. They work all night without a word.

== a3.e10.epee
## The forge
By morning, the sword shines like new. On the guard, Hilde has engraved a lantern.
Hilde: “The metal is his, the lantern is yours. If he sees both, maybe he'll remember.”
Mira: “Thank you, Mum.” Hilde smiles, and says nothing so as not to spoil the moment.

== a3.e10.nev
## The forge
Nev: “The Order has the amber and the ember. The sea-foam is in Salvent. All it lacks now is the azure crystal.”
Nev: “It sleeps in Aerie, in the High Winds. That's where Varen and I were born.”
Kael: “Then we go to Aerie. And this time, we get there first.”

== a3.e10.charrette
## The forge
Hilde: “My cart knows every road, from Lanterval to Salvent. Take it whenever you like.”
? *Hilde's cart*: from the map, you can now go straight to any village you've already visited, without crossing the areas.

== a3.e10.message
Act 3 is over! Aldric's sword has been reforged, and the *Cloud Bridge* leads to the High Winds.
