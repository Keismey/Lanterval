# ---------- Step 1: Cloud Bridge ----------

== a4.e1.titre
Cross the Cloud Bridge

== a4.e1.bouton
Climb to the High Winds

== a4.e1.depart
## Act 4 · The Eye
Above Forgecendre, the road climbs into the clouds. Then there is no road at all: only a rope bridge vanishing into the white.
Nev: “The Cloud Bridge. On the other side is Aerie. That's home.”
Nev: “Well, it was home. Before I left with Varen to join the Order.”

== a4.e1.epee
## Cloud Bridge
Kael draws the reforged sword from its scabbard. The blade shines like clear water, and the lantern engraved on the hilt catches the light.
Kael: “Until now I didn't dare use it. As if it were still his.”
Mira: “It's yours now. My mother doesn't forge for display cases.”
? *Aldric's sword* is in your bag: equip it on Kael in the Team menu. It is reserved for Kael, with a class that wields swords.

== a4.e1.pell
## Cloud Bridge
Halfway across, a canvas glider dives toward the team, chased by raptors and Order soldiers clinging to the ropes.
The glider lands in a crash of planks. A red-haired young man jumps out, a satchel clutched to his chest.
Pell: “Pell, postman of the High Winds! Sorry to drop in on you like this. Literally.” [Fight]

== a4.e1.nouvelles
## Cloud Bridge
Pell: “Thanks. I've just come from Salvent. The Order attacked the port three days ago: they took the sea-foam crystal.”
Sol: “And Naïa? The lighthouse keeper's daughter?”
Pell: “Safe and sound. She hid at Maëlle's with the harbour children. She's the one who gave me the message for you.”
Sol closes his eyes for a moment. His hands are still shaking when he opens them.

== a4.e1.aerie
## Cloud Bridge
At the end of the bridge, the wind parts the clouds. A wooden village clings to the cliff on stilts, with windmills and kites.
Nev: “Aerie. They're all going to stare at me.”
Kael: “Then we'll walk in front of you.”

== a4.e1.message
The *Cloud Bridge* is crossed. *Aerie* appears on the High Winds map.

# ---------- Step 2: Aerie ----------

== a4.e2.titre
Arrive in Aerie

== a4.e2.bouton
Speak to the old monk

== a4.e2.arrivee
## Aerie
Aerie's houses stand on stilts above the void. The walkways creak, the windmills turn, and the wind never stops.
On a square of planks, an old monk in a sky-blue robe is waiting, as if he knew the team would come.

== a4.e2.accueil
## Aerie
Shutters slam one after another. A woman pulls her child inside when she sees Nev.
Someone whispers behind the doors, “Liss's girl. The deserter. Varen's sister.”
Nev: “I warned you. Here, nobody forgets anything. Not even with the mist.”

== a4.e2.tavor
## Aerie
Tavor: “I am Tavor, the last monk of the monastery. Nev fled the Order, and this village doesn't know yet whether that is shame or courage.”
Tavor: “I know. The azure crystal sleeps in the monastery ruins. I will take you there.”
Tavor: “But first, you must see the frescoes. The Order hasn't told you everything about the Eye. Nobody has told you everything.”

== a4.e2.village
## Aerie
Tavor: “Rest at the Nest, at Edda's. Joran will sell you his best: merchants no longer climb up here.”
Tavor: “I'll wait for you on the monastery forecourt. Follow the kite path.”

== a4.e2.message
The *Monastery ruins* appear on the map. Edda's Nest and Joran's shop are open.

# ---------- Steps 3 to 5: Monastery ruins ----------

== a4.e3.titre
Enter the monastery

== a4.e3.bouton
Join Tavor on the forecourt

== a4.e3.parvis
## Monastery ruins
The monastery collapsed into the clouds long ago. All that remains is a forecourt, columns and a great stone door.
Tavor: “The monks sealed this door fifty years ago. The stone guardians are still watching.”
On the roof, two gargoyles open their eyes. [Fight]

== a4.e3.porte
## Monastery ruins
Tavor lays his hand on the door. It opens without a sound, as if it recognised him.
Tavor: “Behind it is the cloister of frescoes. And at the far end, the sanctuary where the azure sleeps.”

== a4.e3.message
The monastery door is open. The *cloister of frescoes* leads to the sanctuary.

== a4.e4.titre
Cross the cloister of frescoes

== a4.e4.bouton
Follow Tavor into the cloister

== a4.e4.cloitre
## Monastery ruins
The cloister is full of mist. Robed shapes glide between the columns, their faces worn away.
Tavor: “My brothers. What's left of them. The mist took them one by one, and they still guard the monastery.” [Cross the cloister]

== a4.e4.fresques
## The cloister of frescoes
On the walls, huge frescoes: an eye open above the world, pouring rivers of light into every village.
Tavor: “The Eye is not a monster. It is the memory of the world. It keeps everything we live, and gives it back when we forget.”
On the next fresco, monks pull a stone eyelid over the Eye. And the mist begins to flow.

== a4.e4.verite
## The cloister of frescoes
Tavor: “Fifty years ago, the Eye opened a little. Some monks were afraid of what it showed. They founded the Order of the Eyelid to close it.”
Tavor: “The mist is the Eye closing. If it closes completely, nobody will ever remember anything again.”
Mira: “And Ysold wants to close it for good. With the four crystals.”
Tavor: “Ysold, and a young man who loved her. His name was Orvan.”

== a4.e4.sanctuaire
## Monastery ruins
Kael: “Orvan… He admitted he co-founded the Order. He didn't tell us he knew what it meant.”
Tavor: “He left the next day. He spent his life lighting lanterns against the mist he had helped to make.”
Before them, the sanctuary door. Behind it, something enormous is breathing.

== a4.e4.message
The frescoes have spoken: the Eye is the *memory of the world*. The azure crystal waits in the *sanctuary*.

== a4.e5.titre
The azure crystal

== a4.e5.bouton
Enter the sanctuary

== a4.e5.serpent
## The sanctuary
In the middle of the sanctuary, the azure crystal floats above an altar. A huge serpent coils around the pillar, its scales blue as the sky.
Tavor: “The Azure Serpent. It has guarded the crystal since the monks left. The mist has driven it mad.”
The Serpent opens its pale eyes and hisses. The wind starts to swirl in the hall.

== a4.e5.conseil
The Azure Serpent resists *Wind* and *Water*. *Earth* and *Fire* split its guard. Break it before it unleashes its storm.

== a4.e5.cristal
## The sanctuary
The Serpent collapses in coils of blue light, and its madness leaves with the wind. The azure crystal drifts gently down toward Nev.
Nev: “It's singing. Like the wind on the peak, when we were little.”

== a4.e5.classes
## The sanctuary
The blue light passes through the team. Kael feels his movements grow lighter, faster.
? New classes: *Sage*, *Ninja* and *Spellblade*. Change class in the Team menu.

== a4.e5.vol
## The sanctuary
A shadow drops from the ceiling and snatches the crystal from Nev's hands. On the ledge, Varen tosses it in his palm.
Varen: “Thanks for doing the work, little sister. The Serpent was the hard part.”
Varen: “If you want it, come and get it on the Howling Peak. Alone. Like when we were kids.”
He vanishes into the wind before Kael can reach the ledge.

== a4.e5.message
Varen has stolen the *azure crystal*. The *Howling Peak* appears on the map.

# ---------- Step 6: Howling Peak ----------

== a4.e6.titre
Catch Varen on the Howling Peak

== a4.e6.bouton
Climb to the summit

== a4.e6.montee
## Howling Peak
The wind howls so loudly in the rocks that you have to shout to be heard. The path zigzags up to the summit, through the ice.
Nev: “We used to come up here with Varen, with our kite. The wind was too strong for the other children.” [Climb to the summit]

== a4.e6.vent
## Howling Peak
Nev stops and looks up at the summit. Her fists are clenched in their wraps.
Nev: “He said alone. He always liked rules, as long as he made them.”

== a4.e6.varen
## The summit
At the top, Varen waits, the azure crystal hanging from his belt. The wind snaps his cloak.
Nev: “Stay back. This is between him and me.”
Kael: “Nev…”
Nev: “Trust me, Kael. For once, I'm the one protecting someone.”

== a4.e6.conseil
Nev fights *alone*. Varen resists *Wind*: strike with *Attack* (Blade), which splits his guard. Keep healing items at hand.

== a4.e6.refus
## The summit
Varen falls to his knees. Nev turns her back to catch her breath, and he picks up his blade.
He raises it, for a moment. Then he lets it fall into the snow.
Varen: “I can't. I never could. You're my sister, Nev.”

== a4.e6.ysold
## The summit
A silver-haired woman steps out of the mist without a sound. Ysold. She tears the crystal from Varen's belt.
Ysold: “You have failed, Varen. I gave you everything, and you choose your sister.”
Ysold: “Keep her. I no longer need you. I have a staircase left to climb.”
She vanishes. Varen doesn't even watch her go.

== a4.e6.ombres
## The summit
The mist closes over the summit. Shadows come out of it, and they are not only looking at the team: they are looking at Varen.
Varen picks up his blade and stands beside Nev.
Varen: “Ysold never leaves witnesses. Let's fight together. Just this once.” [Fight]

== a4.e6.invite
## The summit
The shadows fade into the wind. Varen looks at his hands as if he didn't recognise them.
Nev: “Just this once, huh?”
Varen: “… Maybe twice.”
? *Varen* now fights with the team, in every battle except duels. He isn't in the Team menu and doesn't gain experience: he is always at Kael's level.

== a4.e6.message
*Varen* has changed sides. Ysold has all four crystals. Go back to *Aerie* before the assault.

# ---------- Step 7: the eve of the assault ----------

== a4.e7.titre
The night before the assault

== a4.e7.bouton
Join the fire at the Nest

== a4.e7.feu
## The campfire
Night falls over Aerie. Edda has lit a big fire on the Nest's platform, and for once, the wind is silent.
Tavor: “Ysold has gone up the Stair of Mist, behind the Peak. Up there is the Heart of the Eye.”
Tavor: “Tomorrow, you follow her. Tonight, eat, and talk. Memories are what you're fighting for.”

== a4.e7.kael
## Kael
Kael turns Aldric's sword in the firelight.
Kael: “The Nameless will be up there. My father will be up there. I don't know if I should fight him or bring him home.”
Mira: “Both, maybe. You brought a lantern home once.”

== a4.e7.mira
## Mira
Mira: “Before we left Forgecendre, my mother hugged me. I let her.”
Mira: “I'm still angry with her. But I want to come back so I can stay angry with her for a long time.”

== a4.e7.sol
## Sol
Sol: “Naïa still doesn't know who I am. Or maybe she knows, and she's waiting for me to say it.”
Sol: “If the Eye closes, she'll never remember. So it won't close.”

== a4.e7.nev
## Nev
Varen sits apart, at the edge of the platform. Nev brings him a bowl of soup and sits beside him without a word.
Varen: “Grandmother hasn't spoken to me yet.”
Nev: “She didn't speak to me for three days. Give her time.”

== a4.e7.orvan
## The campfire
Footsteps on the walkway. An old man with a white beard, leaning on a staff, out of breath.
Kael: “Elder Orvan? You climbed all the way up here?”
Orvan: “Fifty years ago, I helped close that Eye. At least let me help you keep it open.”
Orvan: “Tomorrow, I'm coming with you. Don't argue, Kael. Your father never argued either.”

== a4.e7.message
The team has rested. Tomorrow, the *Stair of Mist*.
