== a1.titre
The lantern

# ---------- Prologue ----------

== prologue
## Prologue
Lanterval is a village nestled at the foot of the mountains. For as long as anyone remembers, the lantern on the square has kept the mist away, thanks to the crystal glowing at its heart.
Tonight, its light is flickering.

== prologue.suite
## Prologue
Elder Orvan has sent for you. In the village, a gold *!* marks the main quest and a blue *!* marks a side quest.

# ---------- Step 1: Orvan ----------

== a1.e1.titre
Speak to Elder Orvan

== a1.e1.scene
Orvan: “Kael. The lantern's crystal is dying. Without it, the mist will swallow Lanterval before the season ends.”
Orvan: “Mira, the blacksmith's daughter, left at dawn to follow the mist into the Whispering Forest. Alone, of course. Bring her back, and find out where this evil comes from.”
He breaks a shard off the lantern and hands it to you.
Orvan: “This crystal recognises brave hearts. It will teach you other ways to fight.” [Accept the quest]

== a1.e1.classes
## Classes
*New classes: Warrior, Mage, Healer.* Change class and gear at any time outside combat, with the Team button at the top of the screen.
Each character keeps their element: Kael strikes with Earth whatever his class.

== a1.e1.message
Quest: *find Mira* in the Whispering Forest. Try out the Warrior class.

# ---------- Step 2: Whispering Forest ----------

== a1.e2.titre
Find Mira in the Whispering Forest

== a1.e2.bouton
Find Mira

== a1.e2.traces
Near the path, fresh footprints and charred twigs. Someone has lit flames here, several times. [Follow the tracks]

== a1.e2.encerclee
In a clearing, Mira has her back to a tree and holds three wolves at bay with flames.
Mira: “Kael?! Don't just stand there!” [Help Mira]

== a1.e2.merci
Mira: “Not bad, for an apprentice guard.” She blows on her still-smoking fingers.

== a1.e2.insigne
Something glints in the trampled grass: a metal badge shaped like a closed eye.
Mira: “Those wolves weren't hunting alone. I saw a hooded figure slip between the trees just before they surrounded me.”
Kael: “The Elder will know what it is. Let's head back to the village.”
Mira: “I'm coming with you, and that's not a question.”

== a1.e2.message
Show the badge to *Elder Orvan*, in the village.

# ---------- Step 3: the badge ----------

== a1.e3.titre
Show the badge to the Elder

== a1.e3.scene
You show the badge. The Elder turns pale.
Orvan: “A closed eye… The symbol of the Order of the Eyelid. I thought they had vanished fifty years ago.”
Mira: “And the mist? I followed it all day. It doesn't come from the village: it pours out of the Crystal Cave, north of the forest.”
Orvan: “That's where the crystal heart that feeds our lantern sleeps. If it goes out, nothing will protect us any more.”
Orvan: “Go to the end of the cave and bring me the heart. And beware of those who wear this eye.”

== a1.e3.message
New zone on the map: the *Crystal Cave*, north of the Whispering Forest.

# ---------- Step 4: Sol ----------

== a1.e4.titre
Explore the Crystal Cave

== a1.e4.bouton
Go deeper into the cave

== a1.e4.choix
The main tunnel splits. To the left, an icy draught. To the right, a blue glow slowly pulsing.
> gauche : Left | The draught
> droite : Right | The glow

== a1.e4.gauche
A hollow where cave bats are sleeping. They wake up shrieking! [Fight]

== a1.e4.gauche.fin
Behind them, a miner's satchel: *2 tonics*. The tunnel joins up with the glow.

== a1.e4.droite
You head straight for the glow. The floor is covered in sharp shards: *everyone loses 5 HP*.

== a1.e4.lac
On the shore of an underground lake, a young man with blue hair fends off jellies with his staff. He is surrounded. [Lend a hand]

== a1.e4.sol
Sol: “Thank you. My name is Sol. I've been guarding this lake since the Guardian woke up.”
Sol: “If you want the heart, you'll have to defeat it. It hates water: I've seen it back away from the lake.”

== a1.e4.passage
Sol: “I know a passage along the lake. It leads straight to the far end of the cave.”

== a1.e4.message
You are at the *far end of the cave*. The crystal heart is very close.

# ---------- Step 5: the Guardian ----------

== a1.e5.titre
Defeat the Crystal Guardian

== a1.e5.bouton
Approach the crystal heart

== a1.e5.salle
A vast chamber. In the centre, on a pedestal, the crystal heart beats faintly. The rocks around it begin to tremble…

== a1.e5.gardien
The rocks come together into a colossus. *The Crystal Guardian* blocks your way!

== a1.e5.conseil
Break its guard before it unleashes its charged attack (red “!” badge in the turn order). Auto mode is off against bosses.

== a1.e5.victoire
The Guardian shatters. You pick up the crystal heart: it is warm, like a beating heart. *New class: Knight.*
Behind you, every light in the cave goes out. In the dark, something moves. You'll have to cross the cave again to get out.

== a1.e5.message
The cave has changed: its crystals are dark and other creatures prowl there.

# ---------- Step 6: ambush on the way back ----------

== a1.e6.titre
Leave the cave with the heart

== a1.e6.bouton
Leave the cave

== a1.e6.embuscade
## Ambush
Halfway out, a hooded figure steps out of the shadows.
Hooded figure: “That crystal does not belong to you. Hand it over.” [Refuse]
In reply, two shadows peel away from the walls. [Fight]

== a1.e6.fuite
## Ambush
The figure has vanished into the dark. On its sleeve, you had time to see the same closed eye as on the badge.

== a1.e6.message
You made it out of the cave. Bring the heart back to *Elder Orvan*.

# ---------- Step 7: the celebration ----------

== a1.e7.titre
Bring the heart back to the Elder

== a1.e7.coeur
Orvan: “The heart! You found it!” The Elder takes it in both hands and places it in the lantern's cage.

== a1.e7.fete
## The lantern
The lantern blazes with blue light. The mist retreats to the edge of the forest and all of Lanterval comes out to celebrate.
Reward: *100 gold* and the *Lanterval medallion* (accessory, HP +10). The team is in top shape.

== a1.e7.aveu
Orvan: “Thank you, all three of you. But I must be honest: this heart will only last one season.”
Orvan: “The valley's true crystal, the amber crystal, rests in the Stone Pass, to the north. If the Order is looking for it too, you must reach it before them.”
Mira: “Then we leave tomorrow.” Sol nods without a word.

== a1.e7.message
New path on the map: the *Stone Pass*.
