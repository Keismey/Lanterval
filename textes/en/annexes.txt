# Lanterval · Side quests (English)
# Same format as the French file.

# ---------- 1. The alpha wolf (Brann, Ranger class) ----------

== loup.titre
The alpha wolf

== loup.offre
Brann: “A huge wolf has been leading the packs since the mist started rising. It has a scar over one eye and it has already taken three sheep.”
Brann: “Find it before it finds the village. Come back alive, that's all I ask.”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== loup.message
Side quest: *hunt the alpha wolf* in the forest. Brann advises going as a group.

== loup.e1.titre
Hunt the alpha wolf in the forest

== loup.e1.bouton
Follow the trail

== loup.e1.attente
Brann: “It prowls the heart of the forest. Don't go alone, it has already chased off three hunters.”

== loup.e1.piste
## The alpha wolf
Huge pawprints, bigger than your hand, lead towards a ravine. [Follow the prints]
Near a rock lies the carcass of a deer, barely cold. The wind turns. [Move on]

== loup.e1.ravin
## The alpha wolf
On a rock, under the moon, a dark grey wolf with a scar stares at you. Another wolf leaps out behind you. [Fight]

== loup.e1.fin
## The alpha wolf
The alpha wolf collapses. You take one of its fangs as proof for Brann.

== loup.e1.message
Side quest: go back to *Brann* in the village.

== loup.e2.titre
Go back to Brann

== loup.e2.scene
Brann: “You got it! Your father would have been proud. Take my old bow, I'll show you how a ranger fights.”
## Side quest complete
*Secret class unlocked: Ranger*, very fast, with Double shot to break guards. You also receive *Brann's bow* (only a Ranger can equip it) and *40 gold*.

== loup.apres
Brann: “The sheep sleep soundly thanks to you. If you come across other strange beasts, you know where to find me.”
