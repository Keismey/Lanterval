# ---------- 1. The alpha wolf (Brann, Ranger class) ----------

== loup.titre
The alpha wolf

== loup.offre
Brann: “A huge wolf has been leading the packs since the mist started rising. It has a scar over one eye and it has already taken three sheep.”
Brann: “Find it before it finds the village. Come back alive, that's all I ask.”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== loup.message
Side quest: *hunt the alpha wolf* in the forest. Brann advises going as a group.

== loup.e1.titre
Hunt the alpha wolf in the forest

== loup.e1.bouton
Follow the trail

== loup.e1.attente
Brann: “It prowls the heart of the forest. Don't go alone, it has already chased off three hunters.”

== loup.e1.piste
## The alpha wolf
Huge pawprints, bigger than your hand, lead towards a ravine. [Follow the prints]
Near a rock lies the carcass of a deer, barely cold. The wind turns. [Move on]

== loup.e1.ravin
## The alpha wolf
On a rock, under the moon, a dark grey wolf with a scar stares at you. Another wolf leaps out behind you. [Fight]

== loup.e1.fin
## The alpha wolf
The alpha wolf collapses. You take one of its fangs as proof for Brann.

== loup.e1.message
Side quest: go back to *Brann* in the village.

== loup.e2.titre
Go back to Brann

== loup.e2.scene
Brann: “You got it! Your father would have been proud. Take my old bow, I'll show you how a ranger fights.”
## Side quest complete
? *Secret class unlocked: Ranger*, very fast, with Double shot to break guards. You also receive *Brann's bow* (only a Ranger can equip it) and *40 gold*.

== loup.apres
Brann: “The sheep sleep soundly thanks to you. If you come across other strange beasts, you know where to find me.”

# ---------- 2. The lantern seed (Pia) ----------

== graine.titre
The lantern seed

== graine.offre
Pia: “Grandma says this seed grows a flower that shines in the mist.”
Pia: “It needs salt water, hot ash and a very strong wind. I'm not allowed to leave the village.”
Pia: “Will you look after it? I'm lending it to you. Bring it back as a flower!”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== graine.message
Side quest: the *lantern seed* needs salt water. Maybe in a marsh by the sea?

== graine.e1.titre
Water the seed with salt water

== graine.e1.bouton
Water the seed

== graine.e1.attente
Pia: “Grandma says you find salt water where the sea flows into the marshes. I've never seen the sea.”

== graine.e1.arroser
## The lantern seed
You plant the seed in a hollow of mud and water it with salt water. A small glow lights up under the soil.
Mira: “It's shining! Wait… It's drawing the will-o'-wisps.” [Fight]

== graine.e1.fin
## The lantern seed
With the wisps scattered, a green shoot rises from the mud. A tiny lantern hangs from the tip of its stem.
Sol: “It's still hungry. Hot ash, the little one said. We'll have to wait until we reach Cendre.”

== graine.e1.message
Side quest: the seed has become a *lantern shoot*. *To be continued in act 3*, in hot ash.

== graine.e2.titre
Warm the shoot in the ash (act 3)

== graine.e2.scene
Kael covers the shoot with hot ash. A bud starts to glow.

== graine.e2.attente
Pia: “Has it grown? Grandma says after the water, it needs hot ash. There's lots of it in Cendre, it's in the name!”

== graine.apres
Pia: “Every evening I look at my flower. Grandma says it shines brighter than the lantern. Don't tell her I said that.”

== pnj.pia
Pia: “Grandma says flowers are scared of the mist. I say you just have to find the right one.”

# ---------- 3. The hunter's logbook (Roxane) ----------

== chasse.titre
The hunter's logbook

== chasse.offre
Roxane: “I keep a logbook of the beasts nobody dares to hunt. Four are left, one in each region.”
Roxane: “The first one is close: a boar as big as a cart, up at Stone Pass. Its back is covered in stone.”
Roxane: “Bring me proof of each one, I pay well. And don't be reckless: that one has already sent three hunters home in pieces.”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== chasse.message
Side quest: hunt the *Rock Boar* at *Stone Pass*. Roxane says only Fire can crack its stone guard.

== chasse.e1.titre
Hunt the Rock Boar at Stone Pass

== chasse.e1.bouton
Hunt the Rock Boar

== chasse.e1.attente
Roxane: “The Rock Boar, at Stone Pass. Blades slide off its back. You need fire to split stone.”

== chasse.e1.traque
## The hunter's logbook
Toppled standing stones, deep furrows in the snow. Something very heavy came through here.
Behind a rock, an enormous boar paws at the ground. Its back is covered in plates of stone. [Fight]

== chasse.e1.fin
## The hunter's logbook
The Rock Boar collapses like a rockslide. You pry loose one of its tusks, as long as your arm.
Mira: “Roxane will be pleased. I'm going to ache for a week.”

== chasse.e1.message
Side quest: bring the tusk to *Roxane*.

== chasse.e2.titre
Bring the tusk to Roxane

== chasse.e2.scene
Roxane: “A Rock Boar tusk! I'll write it in the logbook: hunted by Kael, of Lanterval.”
Roxane: “Here's your bounty, 150 coins. The next one is an eel that spits lightning, in the heart of the Lantern Marsh, on the coast.”
Roxane: “It strikes twice as long as its guard holds. Break it fast, or it will break you.”

== chasse.e2.message
Side quest: hunt the *Thunder Eel* in the heart of the *Lantern Marsh*.

== chasse.e3.titre
Hunt the Thunder Eel in the Lantern Marsh

== chasse.e3.bouton
Hunt the Thunder Eel

== chasse.e3.attente
Roxane: “The Thunder Eel lives in the heart of the Lantern Marsh. Earth puts it out, water does nothing to it.”

== chasse.e3.traque
## The hunter's logbook
The black water crackles. Yellow sparks run under the surface, from lantern to lantern.
An eel as long as a rowing boat bursts out of the marsh, its jaws full of sparks. [Fight]

== chasse.e3.fin
## The hunter's logbook
The Thunder Eel falls back into the mud and goes dark. One of its scales still crackles between your fingers.
Sol: “I never thought I'd dive next to one of those. Luckily, we didn't dive.”

== chasse.e3.message
Side quest: bring the scale to *Roxane*.

== chasse.e4.titre
Bring the scale to Roxane

== chasse.e4.scene
Roxane: “A Thunder Eel scale, and you still have all your hair! Two beasts in the logbook.”
Roxane: “Here are 300 coins. The third one lives in the Burnt Fields, at Cendre: a salamander queen who calls her young.”
Roxane: “I'll wait for you there. A hunter always follows the game, and you find plenty of it.”

== chasse.e4.message
Side quest: two beasts out of four. *To be continued in act 3*, in the Burnt Fields.

== chasse.e5.titre
Hunt the Salamander Queen in the Burnt Fields (act 3)

== chasse.e5.scene
Roxane: “The Salamander Queen. Watch out for her young.”

== chasse.e5.attente
Roxane: “The Salamander Queen lives in the Burnt Fields. When the road to Cendre opens, I'll follow you.”

== chasse.apres
Roxane: “Your name is in my logbook four times. Nobody else is in it more than once.”

== pnj.roxane
Roxane: “Don't look at me like that. A scar is just a beast that got lucky once.”

# ---------- 5. The forgotten ship (Rosal) ----------

== navire.titre
The forgotten ship

== navire.offre
Rosal: “I had a ship, I think. The Gull, or was it the Cormorant?”
Rosal: “My head is full of mist, but my hands remember the helm.”
Rosal: “There was a logbook, always kept in the holds. If someone found it, I'd at least know her name.”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== navire.message
Side quest: look for a logbook in the *holds of the Sunken Wreck*.

== navire.e1.titre
Search the holds of the wreck

== navire.e1.bouton
Search the holds

== navire.e1.attente
Rosal: “The holds, kid. What matters is always stowed in the holds.”

== navire.e1.cales
## The forgotten ship
Deep in the holds, burst crates and rotten ropes. Two drowned ones rise from the water, their dead lanterns in hand. [Fight]

== navire.e1.journal
## The forgotten ship
In a watertight crate, a second logbook: “The Salicorne, captain Rosal. Hidden in the lighthouse cove, in case of trouble.”
Mira: “The Salicorne! So she never sank.”

== navire.e1.message
Side quest: look for the *Salicorne* in the cove of the *Drowned Lighthouse*.

== navire.e2.titre
Find the Salicorne in the lighthouse cove

== navire.e2.bouton
Board the Salicorne

== navire.e2.attente
Rosal: “The lighthouse cove… Of course. I hid my ship there whenever the customs men were prowling.”

== navire.e2.crique
## The Salicorne
In the cove, a three-master with grey sails. Smugglers of the Order are loading black cages onto her.
Bosun: “This tub is ours now. Nobody remembers her captain!” [Fight]

== navire.e2.fin
## The Salicorne
The Bosun jumps into the water and swims out to sea. The Salicorne is free, her holds empty of cages.

== navire.e2.message
Side quest: bring the news to *Rosal*, at the Salvent inn.

== navire.e3.titre
Give the Salicorne back to Rosal

== navire.e3.barre
## The Salicorne
Rosal climbs aboard and puts her hands on the helm. Her eyes light up all at once.
Rosal: “The Salicorne! I remember everything. My voyages, my loves, and even my debts.”

== navire.e3.scene
Rosal: “Come here, I'll teach you to fight like a corsair. You strike, you take, and you strike twice as hard when the other one drops their guard.”
## Side quest complete
? *Secret class unlocked: Corsair*, who sometimes steals an item when striking and deals double damage on a broken guard. You receive *Rosal's sabre* (for the Corsair) and *100 gold*.

== navire.apres
Rosal: “When the mist is gone, I'll take you to see the other end of the world. My head remembers it very well now.”

The lost prices

== prix.offre
Gaspard: “How much is a potion? Three coins, three hundred? My ledger has vanished, and my memory with it.”
Gaspard: “I had it on me coming back from the marsh. A magpie jumped on me, and whoosh! No more ledger.”

> non : Later

== prix.e1.titre
Look for the ledger in the Salt Marsh

== prix.e1.bouton
Pick up the pages

== prix.e1.attente
Gaspard: “The Salt Marsh, I think. Or was that yesterday? Anyway, the marsh.”

== prix.e1.pages
## The lost prices
Pages covered in figures lie in the mud between the salt pans. Crabs are using them as a nest. [Fight]

== prix.e1.pie
## The lost prices
You pick up the pages that are left. Above you, a big grey magpie flies off with the ledger's cover in its beak.
Mira: “It's heading north. Towards the Lantern Marsh.”

== prix.e1.message
Side quest: follow the *Mist magpie* to the *Lantern Marsh*.

== prix.e2.titre
Find the Mist magpie's nest

== prix.e2.bouton
Find the magpie's nest

== prix.e2.attente
Gaspard: “A magpie stole my ledger? That explains everything. Well, almost everything.”

== prix.e2.nid
## The lost prices
At the top of a dead tree, a huge nest full of spoons, buckles and coins. The Mist magpie defends its treasure. [Fight]

== prix.e2.fin
## The lost prices
In the nest, the ledger's cover, spoons and three rings nobody will ever claim. You glue the pages back: the ledger is complete.

== prix.e2.message

== prix.e3.titre
Give Gaspard his ledger back

Gaspard: “And I'd forgotten a whole shelf at the back of the shop. Pearl claws, no less!”
## Side quest complete
? Gaspard's shop shows the real prices again, with a *10% discount* for you, and sells *Pearl claws* (Monk fists).

== prix.apres
Gaspard: “Twenty coins a potion, not one more. It's written down now!”
