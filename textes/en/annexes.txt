# Lanterval · Side quests (English)
# Same format as the French file.

# ---------- 1. The alpha wolf (Brann, Ranger class) ----------

== loup.titre
The alpha wolf

== loup.offre
Brann: “A huge wolf has been leading the packs since the mist started rising. It has a scar over one eye and it has already taken three sheep.”
Brann: “Find it before it finds the village. Come back alive, that's all I ask.”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== loup.message
Side quest: *hunt the alpha wolf* in the forest. Brann advises going as a group.

== loup.e1.titre
Hunt the alpha wolf in the forest

== loup.e1.bouton
Follow the trail

== loup.e1.attente
Brann: “It prowls the heart of the forest. Don't go alone, it has already chased off three hunters.”

== loup.e1.piste
## The alpha wolf
Huge pawprints, bigger than your hand, lead towards a ravine. [Follow the prints]
Near a rock lies the carcass of a deer, barely cold. The wind turns. [Move on]

== loup.e1.ravin
## The alpha wolf
On a rock, under the moon, a dark grey wolf with a scar stares at you. Another wolf leaps out behind you. [Fight]

== loup.e1.fin
## The alpha wolf
The alpha wolf collapses. You take one of its fangs as proof for Brann.

== loup.e1.message
Side quest: go back to *Brann* in the village.

== loup.e2.titre
Go back to Brann

== loup.e2.scene
Brann: “You got it! Your father would have been proud. Take my old bow, I'll show you how a ranger fights.”
## Side quest complete
*Secret class unlocked: Ranger*, very fast, with Double shot to break guards. You also receive *Brann's bow* (only a Ranger can equip it) and *40 gold*.

== loup.apres
Brann: “The sheep sleep soundly thanks to you. If you come across other strange beasts, you know where to find me.”

# ---------- 5. The forgotten ship (Rosal) ----------

== navire.titre
The forgotten ship

== navire.offre
Rosal: “I had a ship, I think. The Gull, or was it the Cormorant?”
Rosal: “My head is full of mist, but my hands remember the helm.”
Rosal: “There was a logbook, always kept in the holds. If someone found it, I'd at least know her name.”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== navire.message
Side quest: look for a logbook in the *holds of the Sunken Wreck*.

== navire.e1.titre
Search the holds of the wreck

== navire.e1.bouton
Search the holds

== navire.e1.attente
Rosal: “The holds, kid. What matters is always stowed in the holds.”

== navire.e1.cales
## The forgotten ship
Deep in the holds, burst crates and rotten ropes. Two drowned ones rise from the water, their dead lanterns in hand. [Fight]

== navire.e1.journal
## The forgotten ship
In a watertight crate, a second logbook: “The Salicorne, captain Rosal. Hidden in the lighthouse cove, in case of trouble.”
Mira: “The Salicorne! So she never sank.”

== navire.e1.message
Side quest: look for the *Salicorne* in the cove of the *Drowned Lighthouse*.

== navire.e2.titre
Find the Salicorne in the lighthouse cove

== navire.e2.bouton
Board the Salicorne

== navire.e2.attente
Rosal: “The lighthouse cove… Of course. I hid my ship there whenever the customs men were prowling.”

== navire.e2.crique
## The Salicorne
In the cove, a three-master with grey sails. Smugglers of the Order are loading black cages onto her.
Bosun: “This tub is ours now. Nobody remembers her captain!” [Fight]

== navire.e2.fin
## The Salicorne
The Bosun jumps into the water and swims out to sea. The Salicorne is free, her holds empty of cages.

== navire.e2.message
Side quest: bring the news to *Rosal*, at the Salvent inn.

== navire.e3.titre
Give the Salicorne back to Rosal

== navire.e3.barre
## The Salicorne
Rosal climbs aboard and puts her hands on the helm. Her eyes light up all at once.
Rosal: “The Salicorne! I remember everything. My voyages, my loves, and even my debts.”

== navire.e3.scene
Rosal: “Come here, I'll teach you to fight like a corsair. You strike, you take, and you strike twice as hard when the other one drops their guard.”
## Side quest complete
*Secret class unlocked: Corsair*, who sometimes steals an item when striking and deals double damage on a broken guard. You receive *Rosal's sabre* (for the Corsair) and *100 gold*.

== navire.apres
Rosal: “When the mist is gone, I'll take you to see the other end of the world. My head remembers it very well now.”

# ---------- 7. The lost prices (Gaspard) ----------

== prix.titre
The lost prices

== prix.offre
Gaspard: “How much is a potion? Three coins, three hundred? My ledger has vanished, and my memory with it.”
Gaspard: “I had it on me coming back from the marsh. A magpie jumped on me, and whoosh! No more ledger.”
> oui : Accept | Side quest
> non : Later

== prix.message
Side quest: look for Gaspard's ledger in the *Salt Marsh*.

== prix.e1.titre
Look for the ledger in the Salt Marsh

== prix.e1.bouton
Pick up the pages

== prix.e1.attente
Gaspard: “The Salt Marsh, I think. Or was that yesterday? Anyway, the marsh.”

== prix.e1.pages
## The lost prices
Pages covered in figures lie in the mud between the salt pans. Crabs are using them as a nest. [Fight]

== prix.e1.pie
## The lost prices
You pick up the pages that are left. Above you, a big grey magpie flies off with the ledger's cover in its beak.
Mira: “It's heading north. Towards the Lantern Marsh.”

== prix.e1.message
Side quest: follow the *Mist magpie* to the *Lantern Marsh*.

== prix.e2.titre
Find the Mist magpie's nest

== prix.e2.bouton
Find the magpie's nest

== prix.e2.attente
Gaspard: “A magpie stole my ledger? That explains everything. Well, almost everything.”

== prix.e2.nid
## The lost prices
At the top of a dead tree, a huge nest full of spoons, buckles and coins. The Mist magpie defends its treasure. [Fight]

== prix.e2.fin
## The lost prices
In the nest, the ledger's cover, spoons and three rings nobody will ever claim. You glue the pages back: the ledger is complete.

== prix.e2.message
Side quest: bring the ledger back to *Gaspard*, in Salvent.

== prix.e3.titre
Give Gaspard his ledger back

== prix.e3.scene
Gaspard: “My ledger! A potion is twenty coins. For you, eighteen.”
Gaspard: “And I'd forgotten a whole shelf at the back of the shop. Pearl claws, no less!”
## Side quest complete
Gaspard's shop shows the real prices again, with a *10% discount* for you, and sells *Pearl claws* (Monk fists).

== prix.apres
Gaspard: “Twenty coins a potion, not one more. It's written down now!”
