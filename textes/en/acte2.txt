# Lanterval · Act 2: The Town That Forgets (English texts)
# Same format as acte1.txt (see the help at the top of that file).

== a2.titre
The Town That Forgets

# ---------- Step 1: Salt Marsh ----------

== a2.e1.titre
Cross the Salt Marsh

== a2.e1.bouton
Follow the lost fisherman

== a2.e1.pecheur
## Act 2 · The Town That Forgets
By the salt pans, a man walks in circles, an empty net over his shoulder.
Fisherman: “Hello… I think I was heading home. But I no longer know where that is, or what my name is.”
Sol: “The mist. It takes the little things first, then the rest.”
Mira: “We're going to Salvent. Come with us, someone will recognise you.” [Cross the marsh]

== a2.e1.crabes
## Salt Marsh
Salt crunches underfoot. White shells rise out of the mud. [Fight]

== a2.e1.nom
## Salt Marsh
Fisherman: “Thank you! You know, I feel like my name starts with an L. Or a B.”
Kael: “That's progress.”

== a2.e1.cote
## The coast
At the end of the marsh, the land slopes down to the sea. A harbour shows through the mist, and further out a dark lighthouse on its rock.
Sol stops. He stares at the lighthouse for a long time, without a word.
Fisherman: “Salvent! That one I remember. I think.”

== a2.e1.message
*Salvent* appears on the Coast map. The fisherman follows you.

# ---------- Step 2: Salvent ----------

== a2.e2.titre
Discover Salvent

== a2.e2.bouton
Enter Maëlle's inn

== a2.e2.arrivee
## Salvent
Salvent runs down to the harbour in steps. The boats are moored, the nets are drying, and nobody seems to know why.
A woman drops her basket when she sees the fisherman.
“Loïc! I've been looking for you for three days!”
Loïc: “Loïc… Yes! That's me!”
Loïc: “And you are… my wife? Sorry, it's the mist.”

== a2.e2.gaspard
## Salvent
On the square, a merchant shouts his prices at the crowd.
Gaspard: “Healing herbs, four coins! No, four hundred! Or eighty?”
Mira: “At least we might land a bargain.”

== a2.e2.maelle
## The inn
At the inn, an old woman is drying glasses. She freezes when she sees Sol.
Maëlle: “Those eyes… You're the little one from the shipwreck. The one we thought the sea had taken.”
Sol: “You remember me?”
Maëlle: “I'm too old for the mist to find room in my head. Sit down, little one.”

== a2.e2.lanternes
## The inn
Maëlle: “Three small lanterns used to shine in the marsh to the north. They kept the mist away from the harbour.”
Maëlle: “They went out the day those people with the closed eye took the lighthouse.”
Kael: “The Order. They're here too.”
Maëlle: “Light the lanterns again, and the town will hold for a few days. Then we'll talk about the lighthouse.”

== a2.e2.message
Light the three lanterns of the *Lantern Marsh* again, north of Salvent.

# ---------- Step 3: Lantern Marsh ----------

== a2.e3.titre
Light the three lanterns

== a2.e3.bouton
Light the lanterns

== a2.e3.berge
## Lantern Marsh
The first lantern stands on a post at the edge of the black water. Blue flames dance around it.
Mira: “Will-o'-wisps. They've eaten all the light.” [Fight]

== a2.e3.premiere
## Lantern Marsh
Mira breathes on the wick. *The first lantern lights up* and the mist draws back a few steps.
Sol: “The second one is further on, towards the heart of the marsh.” [Go deeper into the marsh]

== a2.e3.deuxieme
## Lantern Marsh
The second lantern leans among the reeds. Enormous toads stand guard. [Fight]

== a2.e3.deuxieme.fin
## Lantern Marsh
*The second lantern shines.* A path of light opens towards the heart of the marsh.

== a2.e3.coeur
## The heart of the marsh
The last lantern is bent, half in the water. Something long slides around the post.
The water rises. A wyvern spreads wings of moss and hisses.
Kael: “She's the one keeping the mist here.”

== a2.e3.conseil
The *Marsh Wyvern* prepares a breath that hits the whole team. Break her guard with *Earth* (Kael) to stop it. She resists Fire and Water.

== a2.e3.troisieme
## The heart of the marsh
The Wyvern sinks into the mud and does not come back up. Kael straightens the post; Mira lights the wick.
*All three lanterns shine.* In the distance, the harbour of Salvent comes out of the mist.
Sol looks out to sea, towards an old hull run aground.
Sol: “The ship I was wrecked on is out there, under the water. I have to go back.”

== a2.e3.message
The lanterns protect the harbour. The *Sunken Wreck* appears on the map, south of Salvent.

# ---------- Step 4: Sunken Wreck ----------

== a2.e4.titre
Dive into the wreck

== a2.e4.bouton
Dive with Sol

== a2.e4.plongee
## Sunken Wreck
Sol walks into the water without hesitating. A bubble of air forms around the group, held by his power.
Sol: “I never came back. I was six. My sister was two.”
Mira: “We're with you.” [Enter the hull]

== a2.e4.coursive
## Sunken Wreck
The gangway is overgrown with seaweed. Pale shapes move between the cabins. [Fight]

== a2.e4.cabine
## Sunken Wreck
At the end of the gangway, the captain's cabin. The logbook floats, sealed in a copper box.

== a2.e4.journal
## The logbook
“Night of storm. Hull torn open on the lighthouse rocks. A boy swept away by the current, never found.”
“A girl of two saved from the water and taken in at Salvent.”
Sol holds the logbook close.
Sol: “She's alive. She's been in Salvent all along.”

== a2.e4.mere
## The hold
The floor shakes. In the hold, a shell covered in barnacles rises: the Abyssal Mother guards her nest.

== a2.e4.conseil
The *Abyssal Mother* has a huge guard and calls crabs to help. Hit her with *Earth* or *Fire*; Blade and Water slide off her shell.

== a2.e4.fin
## The hold
The Abyssal Mother backs into a hole in the hull and disappears.
Kael: “Let's go back. Maëlle will know who took your sister in.”

== a2.e4.message
Go back to *Maëlle* at the inn in Salvent.

# ---------- Step 5: Naïa ----------

== a2.e5.titre
Find Sol's sister

== a2.e5.bouton
Show the logbook to Maëlle

== a2.e5.maelle
## The inn
Maëlle reads the logbook page. Her hands tremble a little.
Maëlle: “The little girl was taken in by Erwan. The lighthouse keeper. He named her Naïa.”
Maëlle: “The Order is holding Erwan in the lighthouse. Naïa waits for him on the quay every evening.” [Go to the quay]

== a2.e5.naia
## The quay
A girl in a yellow oilskin looks at the dark lighthouse. She has the same blue hair as Sol.
Naïa: “Are you travellers? My father is over there. Those people locked him up to keep the light.”
Naïa: “They told me I had a family, before. But I don't remember anything. The mist, I suppose.”
Sol opens his mouth, then closes it.

== a2.e5.sol
## The quay
Mira: “You're not telling her?”
Sol: “Not now. She has a father, and he's a prisoner. I won't give her a brother and one more sorrow.”
Sol: “We free Erwan first. The lighthouse is on the rock, to the west.”

== a2.e5.message
The *Drowned Lighthouse* appears on the map. The Order holds it.

# ---------- Step 6: Drowned Lighthouse, the cove ----------

== a2.e6.titre
Get into the lighthouse

== a2.e6.bouton
Reach the lighthouse stairs

== a2.e6.crique
## Drowned Lighthouse
The sea comes up to the lighthouse door. Banners with a closed eye snap in the wind.
Soldiers of the Order guard the entrance. [Fight]

== a2.e6.nev
## The stairs
On the first steps, a novice of the Order raises her spear. She isn't even sixteen.
Nev: “Halt! I have to… I have to raise the alarm.”
She looks at Sol, then at the harbour, then at her own hands.
Nev: “They said we came to save Salvent from its memories. But people don't even know their children's names any more.”
Nev: “I didn't see you. The Herald is at the top. Hurry.”

== a2.e6.escalier
## The stairs
Halfway up, two soldiers come down the steps. They didn't pass Nev. [Fight]

== a2.e6.escalier.fin
## The stairs
The steps keep turning. At the very top, a cold light seeps under a door.

== a2.e6.sommet
## The top
The lighthouse lamp is covered by a black cage. Inside, a sea-foam coloured crystal beats like a heart.
An old man is tied to the railing: Erwan, the keeper.

== a2.e6.message
The *Herald of the Eyelid* awaits you at the top of the lighthouse.

# ---------- Step 7: Drowned Lighthouse, the top ----------

== a2.e7.titre
Face the Herald

== a2.e7.bouton
Challenge the Herald

== a2.e7.heraut
## The Herald of the Eyelid
Herald: “The children of the lantern. Varen told me about you.”
Herald: “Look at this harbour. No one here mourns their dead any more. We offer it peace, and you want to give it back its grief.”
Kael: “You're taking their memories. That isn't peace, it's emptiness.”
Herald: “Then close your eyes with them.”

== a2.e7.conseil
The *Herald* prepares “Eyelid Shut”, a blow on the whole team. Break his guard with *Water* (Sol) or *Earth* (Kael) to cancel it. He resists Fire.

== a2.e7.cristal
## The sea-foam crystal
The Herald falls to his knees, then flees through the window, into a boat of the Order.
Kael breaks the cage. The sea-foam crystal lights up, and the lighthouse casts its beam over the sea.
As in Lanterval, the crystal answers the lantern shard. It teaches you new ways to fight.

== a2.e7.gardien
## The top
Erwan: “Thank you, children. My little Naïa must be worried sick.”
Sol unties him without a word. His hands are shaking a little.
Erwan: “Let's go back to Salvent. Tonight, the lighthouse keeps watch again.”

== a2.e7.message
The lighthouse shines. Go back to *Maëlle*'s inn in Salvent.

# ---------- Step 8: Salvent, Orvan's letter ----------

== a2.e8.titre
Return to Salvent

== a2.e8.bouton
Celebrate the return of the light

== a2.e8.ville
## Salvent
Three days have passed. The mist no longer gets past the rocks, and the fishermen remember their first names.
Loïc: “I've found my boat, my wife and my children. In that order, but I won't tell them.”
Erwan and Naïa sit at a table in the inn. Maëlle has brought out her best soup.

== a2.e8.berceuse
## The inn
Naïa hums as she stirs her soup. Sol freezes.
Sol: “That song… Where did you learn it?”
Naïa: “I don't know. It's always been in my head. Nobody else knows it.”
Sol smiles without answering. Their mother sang it every night.

== a2.e8.vision
## A vision
That night, under the lighthouse beam, the lantern shard starts to glow in your hand.
A vision: a woman with a calm gaze, and beside her a smiling young man. Behind them, a huge eye carved in stone.
Kael: “That young man… it's Orvan. Orvan, at twenty.”
The next day, you hand a letter for Orvan to the valley coach.

== a2.e8.lettre
## The inn
Maëlle: “Kael! The valley coach brought a letter for you. The handwriting shakes a little.”

== a2.e8.aveu
## Orvan's letter
“Kael, you saw right. Fifty years ago, I founded the Order of the Eyelid with Ysold. We wanted no one to suffer ever again.”
“Ysold lost her whole family in the war. She wanted to close the Eye, the source of the world's memory.”
“I was afraid, and I left. I never told you. Forgive me.”
“Brann's scouts are certain: the amber crystal from the pass was taken to the forges of Cendre, in the east.”
Mira: “Cendre… My mother's town.”

== a2.e8.est
## Heading east
Sol: “I'm staying with you. Naïa has Erwan, and the lighthouse. I'll come back to talk to her when the mist is gone.”
Kael: “Then we set off again. The forges of Cendre, in the east.”

== a2.e8.message
Act 2 is over! The sea-foam crystal protects Salvent, and the road to Cendre goes through Lanterval.
