## Title
Lanterval: Watercolor RPG

## Short description
Relight your village's lantern in a hand-painted, turn-based RPG.

## Full description
The lantern of Lanterval is dying. Each night its light grows a little weaker, and the mist creeps over the fields. Kael, a seventeen-year-old apprentice guard, has never left the valley. Now he has to set out and bring the lantern back to life.

A STORY IN FOUR ACTS
• The Lantern, The Town That Forgets, The Forges of Cendre, The Eye: a complete story with a real beginning, middle and end.
• Kael, Mira the blacksmith's daughter, Sol the hermit of the underground lake, Nev the runaway novice: four heroes, each with their own past, doubts and epilogue.
• A valley, a mist-drowned coast, forges beneath the volcano, towns perched in the winds, and a stair that climbs all the way to the Eye.

BATTLES THAT MAKE YOU THINK
• Turn-based battles with five elements: Blade, Earth, Fire, Water and Wind.
• Break your enemies' guard by hitting their weaknesses, then make the most of the opening.
• 20 classes to unlock and swap whenever you like: Warrior, Mage, Healer, Knight, Monk, Thief, Bard, Ranger, Corsair, Ninja, Spellblade, Dragoon and many more.
• Bosses that fly into a rage, shield themselves or change the rules: you'll have to adapt your party.
• Auto mode and fast battles for easy fights, when you just want to move on.

A WORLD TO EXPLORE
• 19 places to explore, over 70 monsters and nearly a hundred pieces of gear.
• Side quests that follow your heroes from act to act, family memories to find, a Codex to fill.
• After the ending, New Game+: start again with everything you've earned and face the Echoes, stronger versions of the great bosses, each guarding a unique item.

HAND-PAINTED
• Every background, character and monster is painted in watercolor.
• Original music for each region.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
• About 6 hours for the full story, much more to complete everything.
• Normal difficulty or Story mode, if you're mostly here for the adventure.
• Plays offline, no account, no ads.
• Your save stays on your phone, and you can export it as a code.
• In English and French.

A game by Keismey Studio, a one-person independent studio.
